➕Add Vocabulary
Paste a list of words (one per line, or comma-separated) or upload a .txt file — Focci will look each one up with AI and save it to your library. For big batches (hundreds+), use the separate generator tool instead.
🔍Meaning Quality Scan
Checks your entries for Vietnamese that carries the wrong feeling, reads like machine translation, or misses a common meaning. Only the word and its Vietnamese are sent, batched 40 at a time — so this is cheap. Progress is saved, so you can stop and continue any day.

✎Rewrite Weak Meanings
Regenerates only the Vietnamese side of flagged entries — collocations, idioms, phonetics and examples are kept, so each word costs about a quarter of a full rebuild. Updates land straight in your library; nothing to download or re-upload.

Already have your results? Upload them instead of scanning again — a .txt with one word per line, an audit.csv (word,reason), or a JSON array. Words not in your library are skipped.
🧭Find Missing Words
Compares your library against a common-English frequency list, drops anything you already have (including verb forms and word families), then screens the rest with AI to remove names, abbreviations and junk. The survivors queue up — add as many or as few as you want, whenever you want.

💾Backup
Downloads every word in your library as one JSON file. Keep a copy somewhere safe — if this device is wiped or the browser clears its storage, this file is the only way back.

🔒Data & Privacy
